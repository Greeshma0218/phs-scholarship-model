PHS Award Model Simulator

What-if modeling for Presidential Honors Scholarship award criteria

Model rules
  • GPA and test score awards stack when the two levels are within a set number of levels of each other (adjust the window below).
  • If the two levels are further apart than that window, the student receives the single higher applicable award (no stacking).
  • Students at GPA Level 1 and Test Level 1 qualify for President's Inner Circle (PIC) and receive a flat award, overriding the stacking/cap logic above.
