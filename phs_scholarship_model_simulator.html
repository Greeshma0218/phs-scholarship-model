PHS Option 1 model simulator — Fall 2026 FTIC

Wider stacking window, revised award tables, lower PIC amount — applied to the actual Fall 2026 deposited FTIC class

Model rules
  • GPA and test score awards stack when the two levels are within two levels of each other (|GPA level − Test level| ≤ 2).
  • If the two levels are more than two apart, the student receives the single higher applicable award (no stacking).
  • Students at GPA Level 1 and Test Level 1 qualify for President's Inner Circle (PIC) and receive a flat award, overriding the stacking/cap logic above.
